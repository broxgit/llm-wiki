confluence     off. Watch Confluence pages and tell your wiki when one changes. It can also publish a wiki to a space, if you choose. Turn it on: wiki extensions add confluence
jira           off. Check the Jira tickets your open items wait on, and close the ones that are done. Turn it on: wiki extensions add jira

An extension is an executable named wiki-<name> in ~/.local/share/llm-wiki/extensions.
wiki <name> runs it. docs/extensions.md says what it is given.
